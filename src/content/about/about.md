Helen Popich Harris is a Lafayette family law attorney whose practice is devoted to the financial dimensions of divorce: complex community property partitions, high-value marital estates, spousal support, retirement division, and child support. She is a Board Certified Family Law Specialist, certified by the Louisiana Board of Legal Specialization, and a Fellow of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers.

Both credentials are conferred only after independent review. In Louisiana, board certification in family law requires substantial experience in the specialty, peer review, a written examination, and continuing education focused on family law. Fellowship in the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers is by selection; the Academy's Fellows are attorneys whose practices are concentrated in matrimonial and family law. Ms. Harris is also listed by the Academy as a Certified Arbitrator. [[CONFIRM: mention the arbitrator credential?]]

Helen earned her Juris Doctor from Loyola University New Orleans School of Law and was admitted to the Louisiana bar in 1991. She holds an undergraduate degree in business administration from the University of Southwestern Louisiana, now the University of Louisiana at Lafayette [[CONFIRM]] — a grounding that serves her well in cases built on balance sheets, business valuations, and retirement plans.

## How she works

Ms. Harris keeps her practice deliberately small so that she can know each client's circumstances in detail and respond to them personally. For many clients, a divorce involving a business, a professional practice, or substantial retirement savings is the most consequential financial event of their lives. She treats it that way: explaining options in plain language, building the financial record with care, and resolving matters by agreement when that serves the client — while preparing to litigate when it does not.

She has also taught other professionals, speaking at continuing legal education programs on community property partitions, reimbursement claims, ethics, and conflicts of interest, and addressing the Louisiana Society of CPAs on the accountant's contribution in a divorce case.

Helen practices from her office on West Main Street in downtown Lafayette and represents clients throughout Acadiana. [[CONFIRM: parishes served and any local roots she would like mentioned]]

Her work has been recognized by peers and independent publications: she has been selected to Louisiana Super Lawyers® for 2022, 2025 and 2026, holds an AV Preeminent® Peer Review Rating from Martindale-Hubbell, and was named to Acadiana Profiles Top Lawyers 2025 in family law.
