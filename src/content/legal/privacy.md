This policy explains what information this website collects and how Helen Popich Harris, APLC ("the firm," "we") uses it.

## Information you choose to send

If you use the inquiry form, we receive the information you enter: your name, telephone number, email address, preferred contact method, the type of matter, the name of the other party (if you provide it), and your message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, to check for conflicts of interest, and to arrange a consultation. We do not sell or rent it, and we do not use it for marketing lists.

Please do not send confidential or sensitive details through the form. Submitting an inquiry does not create an attorney–client relationship.

## How the form is processed

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Form submissions are received and stored by Netlify and forwarded to the firm by email. Netlify's handling of data is described in its own privacy policy. We retain inquiries only as long as needed to respond and to maintain conflict-checking records, consistent with our professional obligations.

## Information collected automatically

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. Like most web hosts, our hosting provider may record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) in server logs for security and reliability.
